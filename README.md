# About AI-lesson

*Artificial Intelligence is computer technology that allows machines to learn, reason, and perform tasks like humans.*

# What is AI?

## AI uses powerful computer programs and large amounts of data. Instead of following a strict set of rules, AI looks for patterns. It learns from past mistakes and successes to get better over time.

# Common Uses of AI

- Answering questions: Chatbots talk with people and write text.

- Making recommendations: Streaming apps suggest movies or music you might enjoy based on your history.

- Recognizing images and voice: Phones unlock using your face, and smart speakers listen to your voice commands.

- Driving cars: Self-driving vehicles use AI to see the road and avoid crashes

- Helping in healthcare: AI scans medical pictures to help doctors find diseases early.

# Why AI is Useful ?

- Speed: It processes huge amounts of data in seconds.

- Automation: It takes over repetitive or dangerous jobs.

- Availability: Digital assistants work twenty-four hours a day without getting tired.
